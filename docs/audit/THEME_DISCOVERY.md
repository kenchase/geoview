### Questions the source does not settle

- Whether literal transition curves/delays or the slider's overshoot are deliberate;
  numerical duration equality does not establish interchangeable behavior.
- Whether repeated edge-shadow geometry expresses elevation, separation or another
  role. The global Paper override and component-specific shadows overlap.
- Whether unequal external-theme font fallbacks, raw text/icon sizes and custom
  `control` typography are intentional. The custom-legend header size is user input.
- The tab-navigation hook defaults its delay to `TIMEOUT.shortcutToTab`, which is
  absent from that object's initializer; its comment says 350ms.
  [TIMEOUT](../../packages/geoview-core/src/core/utils/constant.ts#L170) is declared
  `Record<string, number>`, but the missing property evaluates to `undefined`.
  Without a caller-supplied delay, `setTimeout(fn, undefined)` queues a zero-delay
  task (about 0ms, subject to event-loop scheduling and timer clamping), not a
  350ms wait. Intent is unknown.
  This is recorded without changing it or attributing it to a normalization regression.
- Static CSS and colour-picker `cssText` do not read the per-map theme at those
  assignments. Portaled controls, external themes and held colour/shadow pairings
  remain distinct consumers; no new token or CSS-variable mechanism is implied.
